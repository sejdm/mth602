Exercise sheet 1

 Last modified: 09:59 AM - 28 August, 2026

PDF version

  1. What is \(\pi_1(X \times Y, (x_0,y_0))\) in terms of \(\pi_1(X, x_0)\), and \(\pi_1(Y, y_0)\)?

  2. Show that \(f_*\) is injective where \(f: S^1 \to S^1 \times S^1\) defined as \(f(t)=(s_0,t)\).

  3. Find a covering map \(p : S^1 \to S^1\) which is \(n\)-sheeted, which means that the fibre has \(n\) points.

  4. Show that \(\mathbb{R}\times \mathbb{R}\) is a cover of \(S^1 \times S^1\) and use that to compute the fundamental group of \(S^1 \times S^1\).

  5. Show that if \(p_1 : X \to Y\) and \(p_2 : Y \to Z\) are covering maps, then \(p_2 \circ p_1\) is a covering map.

  6. Show that \(p : X \to Y\) is a covering map, both \(X\) and \(Y\) are path connected, and \(Y\) is simply connected, then \(p\) is a homeomorphism.
