  1. If \(f : X \to Y\) and \(g : Y \to Z\) be covering maps. Show that the composition \(g \circ f\) is a covering map if \(Z\) has a universal cover.

  2. Prove that the fundamental group of a graph is always free. (Hint: Consider the maximal tree)

  3. Prove that the cover of a graph is also a graph.

  4. Use the previous two exercises to show that the algberaic fact that the subgroup of a free group is always free.

  5. A cover whose deck transformation group is abelian is called an abelian cover. Prove that for a path connected, locally path connected, and semi-locally simply connected, there exists a “universal abelian cover”, i.e. an abelian cover that covers all other abelian covers.
