  1. Given a covering \(p : X \to Y\), if \(B \subset Y\) and \(A = p^{-1}(B)\), then prove that the restriction \(p : A \to B\) is also a covering.

  2. Find the deck transformation groups of the coverings: \(p : \mathbb{R} \to S^1\), and \(p_2 : S^2 \to \mathbb{RP}^2\).

  3. Find all the covers of \(\mathbb{RP}^2\) and \(S^1\).

  4. Find a simply connected cover of the wedge of two circles. (Hint: Try lifting the circles)

  5. Find a cover \(p : X \to S^1 \vee S^1\) of the wedge of two circles so that the image of \(p_*\) is \(\mathbb{Z}\).

  6. If \(X\) is any topological space with a finite fundamental group, then show that any \(f : X \to S^1\) must be homotopic to the constant map.
