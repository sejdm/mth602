  1. Prove that a constant map induces a trivial map on homology.

  2. Use excision to show that \(H_n(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^n \setminus x) \cong H_n(U, U \setminus x)\) for any open \(U\subset X\). Use his to prove that if \(U \subset \mathbb{R}^n\) and \(V \subset \mathbb{R}^m\) are both open and homeomorphic then \(m=n\)

  3. Given a topological space \(X\), let \(SX\) (called the suspension) denote the space \(X \times [0,1]/\sim\) where \((x,t) \sim (y,t)\) if \(t=0\) or \(t=1\). In other words, \(X\times {0}\) is collapsed to a point and \(X \times {1}\) is collapsed to a point. Prove that \(\widetilde{H}_{n+1}(SX) = \widetilde{H}_n(X)\)

  4. Consider that antipodal map \(\theta : S^{n} \to S^{n}\). Prove that \(\theta_*(x) = (-1)^{n+1}x\) where \(x \in H_n(S^n)\) is a generator.
